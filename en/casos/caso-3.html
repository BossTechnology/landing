Pinch Jobs: hundreds of simultaneous jobs, under one command

ServicesCleaningField operations
Industry
Cleaning services
Region
United States
Focus
Field operations, from order to payment
Products
BzzzBX, Metamap, Actionables, Applied AI

In a service company, the business happens on the street: every job is an order, a crew on the move and a payment to confirm. Pinch Jobs now sees that whole journey in real time, and runs it with AI.

The challenge

Hundreds of jobs, many moving parts

Every week, Pinch Jobs coordinates hundreds of cleaning services: incoming requests, customer confirmations, a schedule that keeps changing, crews moving between addresses and payments that need to close.

Each piece lived in its own tool. Knowing how the day was going, which job was running late or which payment was still pending meant cross-checking information by hand, usually too late.

To grow without losing control, leadership needed to see the whole operation, from order to payment, as one system.

Every service is a promise to a customer. You need to see it being kept, not find out afterwards.

The approach

From order to payment, in a single view

With BzzzBX as the core and Metamap as the operations center, Pinch Jobs manages every work order, the schedule, confirmations, where each crew is and when the service is done in one place, down to the last step: payment confirmation.

Day to day

What operations sees and does

  • Work ordersEvery request with its status, from the moment it arrives until it closes.
  • Schedule and confirmationsWhich services are confirmed, which changed and which need a call to the customer.
  • Live crewsWhere each crew is, which job it is on and whether it is on time.
  • Service completedEvery job closed as it happens, with its evidence.
  • PaymentsWhich services are paid, which are on the way and which need follow-up.
  • One viewThe whole day’s operation on one map and one dashboard, no tool-hopping.

Applied AI

From a question to an automatic action

On the same contextualized data, Pinch Jobs connected its AI to BzzzBX through a governed bridge. The team asks questions in natural language, builds its own views and scenarios, and gets answers backed by evidence.

Which 10 customers have the highest outstanding balance, and since when?
These 10 customers carry the largest receivable this month; three have services completed over 30 days ago with no confirmed payment. Here is the list, sorted, with the last service and payment status.Evidence: work orders, service closures and payment systems · illustrative example

When a question becomes routine, like the customers who owe the most or the crews with the most reschedules, it stops being an AI query: it becomes a BzzzBX Actionable that runs on its own, notifies the owner and triggers the action.

One-off query
  1. Someone asks
  2. AI analyzes
  3. Answer on the spot
With Actionables
  1. Pattern detected
  2. Runs automatically
  3. Owner alerted
  4. Action executed

Lower AI cost. AI solves a process the first time; once it becomes recurring, BzzzBX turns it into an Actionable that runs without consuming models. AI spend stops growing with every user and goes where it adds judgment.
